### Phase 3: Best-in-Class Voice (High)
- FEAT-50 Custom Stotra Voice Model — fine-tune Piper/VITS on pre-recorded stotra
  audio (1–3 hrs), export ONNX (~50 MB), integrate as Sherpa-ONNX engine for verse

  align transcripts → train on Colab A100 → export ONNX → load in TtsEngineManager
  as highest-priority verse engine. Also wire ProsodyEngine-aware edge-tts SSML for
  verse as intermediate improvement before custom model is ready.
  Data: user-provided WAV recordings of stotras matching stotras/ text files.
  Pipeline: custom-tts-voice-model/scripts/ (preprocess, train, evaluate, synthesize).
- FEAT-51 Real-time waveform/prosody preview before generation
- FEAT-52 Per-sentence regeneration from preview
- FEAT-53 Dual-engine A/B comparison (gTTS vs Edge-TTS vs Custom side-by-side)
